Why Districts After Dark?

Policy off the dais, culture on the street—one summer night at a time.

Districts After Dark is a ward-by-ward nightcrawl that brings City Councilmembers, neighbors, and small-business owners into the same room (and the same line at the bar). We pair honest civic dialogue with the real places that make New Orleans after dark: music rooms, corner bars, kitchens, and patios.

Each crawl is part town hall, part celebration: trivia that rewards local knowledge, a channel for anonymous questions, and routes built to spotlight businesses that keep our corridors alive. The goal is simple—show up for your ward, meet the people who represent you, and leave with a stronger sense of what your district can be when the sun goes down.
