# Districts After Dark: Influencer Posting Schedule

A realistic 3-week framework for each district influencer, beginning 3 weeks before their Nightcrawl event.

**Role:** Neutral civic host / MC—not a competitive team captain. The influencer drives participation and supports local businesses without picking favorite venues or pitting districts against each other.

**Cadence:** ~1 post per day, with roughly **30–40% of weekly content** devoted to Districts After Dark so the campaign stays visible without overtaking their feed.

**Promotional goals (every post should tie to at least one):**
1. General promotion of the event series and why it’s happening
2. Ways for residents to get involved (vote for Stop 3, submit/vote on councilmember questions, Summer Nights bingo)
3. When everything is happening

**Assumption:** Nightcrawl event on **Day 21**.

---

## Week 1: The Introduction & The “Why” (3 Weeks Out)

*Goal: Announce the partnership, explain the mission, and introduce the timeline.*

| Day | Format | Content | Messaging (Goals) |
|-----|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Day 1** | In-feed post + Story | High-quality photo of influencer and councilmember at a recognizable local spot | *“I’m stepping out of my usual lane to team up with Councilmember [Name] for Districts After Dark on [Date]! This isn’t a boring town hall—we’re hitting the streets to support our nightlife and have real conversations about our ward. Save the date!”* **(1 & 3)** |
| **Day 3** | Story | Quick selfie-video or graphic | *“Before we even crawl, you can start earning points. Download the Summer Nights Bingo card at the link in my bio. Go buy a drink at a local spot, tip your bartender, and start checking off boxes to win prizes at the event!”* **(2)** |
| **Day 6** | Story | Graphic map of the district | *“Here’s how [Date] is going to work. Stop 1 is [Venue A]. Stop 2 is [Venue B]… but Stop 3 is completely blank. Next week, YOU get to vote on where we end the night.”* **(3)** |

**Days 2, 4, 5, 7:** Normal influencer content (no DAD post required).

---

## Week 2: The Run-Off Phase (Top 5)

*Goal: Narrow the field to the Top 5 and drive high-stakes run-off voting.*

| Day | Format | Content | Messaging (Goals) |
|-----|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Day 8 (Monday 3PM)** | In-feed Reel/Video | Fast-paced walk past the Top 5 nominated venues | *“The Run-Off is officially here! We've narrowed it down to the Top 5 venues for our final stop. I’m keeping my vote a secret, but you have 48 hours to cast yours at the link in my bio!”* **(2)** |
| **Day 10** | Story (Instagram Q&A sticker) | Native Question Box | *“At Stop 2, I’m sitting down with Councilmember [Name]. What do you actually want to know about our district? Drop your questions here, and I’ll take the best ones to the official voting page for everyone to rank.”* **(2)** |
| **Day 13** | Story | Screenshot of current voting leaderboard (counts only—no endorsement) | *“Voting for the 3rd stop closes TOMORROW! It’s crazy close between the Top 5. Also, go to the site and upvote the questions you want me to ask the councilmember!”* **(2 & 3)** |
| **Day 14** | Story | Official graphic announcing winning Stop 3 venue | *“The votes are in! We are ending the night at [Winning Venue]! See you there next week.”* **(3)** |

**Days 9, 11, 12:** Normal influencer content (optional: light Story reminder to vote if engagement is low).

---

## Week 3: Logistics & Final Hype (Week of the Event)

*Goal: Final RSVP push, question hype, and event-day coverage.*

| Day | Format | Content | Messaging (Goals) |
|-----|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Day 16** | In-feed carousel | Slide 1: 3 stops + times. Slide 2: Top 3 community questions. Slide 3: Bingo card | *“This [Day]! Here is exactly where we will be and when. Swipe to see the top questions you all voted for me to ask the councilmember. Bring your Bingo cards!”* **(1, 2 & 3)** |
| **Day 19** | Story | Casual “getting ready for the weekend” video | *“Just a reminder that Districts After Dark is this [Day]. It’s free, but RSVP at the link in my bio so the bars know how many bartenders to staff!”* **(3)** |
| **Day 21** | Stories (throughout event) | Live coverage | See **Event Day** below |

**Days 17, 18, 20:** Normal influencer content (optional: bingo reminder Story on Day 18).

### Event Day (Day 21) — Story sequence

| Time | Story |
|------|--------|
| **~6:00 PM** | *“We are at Stop 1! Place is already packed.”* |
| **~7:30 PM** | Snippets of community-voted questions with the councilmember at Stop 2 |
| **~9:00 PM** | *“We made it to Stop 3! You guys picked a great spot to end the night. Come cash in your bingo cards!”* |

## At-a-glance: Required vs. optional touchpoints

| Week | In-feed posts | Stories | Focus |
|------|---------------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 1 (Day 1) | 2 (Days 3, 6) | Why + timeline + bingo |
| 2 | 1 (Day 8) | 3 (Days 10, 13, 14) | Vote + questions + reveal |
| 3 | 1 (Day 16) | 1 pre-event + event-day Stories | Itinerary + RSVP + live |

**Total:** 3 in-feed posts + ~8–10 Stories over 21 days.

---

## Neutrality guidelines for influencers

- Do **not** endorse a specific venue for Stop 3 (“vote for my favorite bar”).
- Do **not** campaign against other districts or duos.
- **Do** celebrate the process: voting, questions, bingo, and showing up for local businesses.
- **Do** use “we” language (community, ward, district) rather than “my pick” or “our team.”
- When sharing leaderboard screenshots, report facts only—no “help us win” framing.

---

## Why this framework works

1. **Respects their feed:** Only 3 permanent in-feed posts; most touchpoints are Stories that expire in 24 hours.
2. **Interactive before the link:** Q&A sticker (Day 10) engages followers before they visit the site.
3. **Neutral host role:** Drives democratic participation and civic engagement, not a single outcome or rival district.

---

## Customization per district

Replace placeholders before sharing with each influencer:

- `[Name]` — Councilmember name  
- `[Date]` / `[Day]` — Event date  
- `[Venue A]`, `[Venue B]` — Confirmed Stops 1 & 2  
- `[N]` — Number of Stop 3 nominees  
- `[Venue C]`, `[Venue D]` — Top two in leaderboard (Day 13 only; update daily if used)  
- `[Winning Venue]` — Announced winner (Day 14)  
- Link in bio → district voting / RSVP / bingo URLs
